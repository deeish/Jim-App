# What's New archive

Release cards pruned from `frontend/src/constants/changelog.ts`. The in-app
"What's New" keeps only the last few releases (see the policy comment in that
file); when a card is pruned, it moves here so the full history stays greppable
without git archaeology. Newest first.

## 2026-08-13 · Version 1.1.0 — Programs that fit your week

- **New** — Two new coach-built programs: Beginner Full Body, your first eight weeks of barbells, and Home Dumbbell Full Body, which needs only dumbbells and a bench.
- **New** — Every program now adjusts to your schedule. Run the 6 day Push/Pull/Legs on 4 days and the sessions rotate through your week automatically.
- **New** — Jim score: our quality rating for every exercise, right on its page.
- **Improved** — Applying a program that starts next Monday now opens on its first week, so you can see everything you are about to train.
- **Fixed** — The saved workouts page no longer sits under the clock.

## 2026-08-11 · Version 1.1.0 — A smarter exercise library

- **Improved** — Every exercise was reviewed and ranked. Look for the Recommended star, filter to just those, and plans now lead with the best options.
- **New** — A redesigned exercise page: key facts at a glance, clearer muscle maps, and everything in easy cards.
- **New** — Exercise pages suggest easier and harder versions you can jump between.
- **New** — The common mistakes for each exercise, with the fix.
- **New** — Replacements respect limitations like a sore shoulder or knee.
- **Improved** — A refreshed Plan tab with week progress, rest days, and quick actions.

## 2026-07-16 · Version 1.0.0 — Share workouts with your gym buddy

- **New** — Share your plan with your gym partner. The feature is on the Plan page once a workout is generated: send a code or have them scan your QR code.

## 2026-07-15 · Version 1.0.0 — Smarter workout generation and search

- **Improved** — Generated workouts now balance pushing and pulling and avoid stacking similar movements.
- **Improved** — Every session fits the time you picked, including your hardest weeks.
- **Improved** — Plans covering several weeks now build up, peak, and finish with a lighter recovery week, keeping the same core lifts throughout.
- **Improved** — Exercise search now understands everyday spellings, typos, and gym slang. Searches like "pullup", "dumbell press", or "ohp" find the right exercise instead of coming up empty.
- **Improved** — A simpler goal step when setting up your profile.
- **New** — Clear an entire week from your plan, with a quick confirmation before a repeated week starts.
- **Fixed** — Saving a workout with the heart now responds instantly and reliably adds it to your saved list.
- **Fixed** — Plan controls display correctly on smaller screens.

## 2026-07-07 · Version 1.0.0 — Weight tracking, a better exercise library, and plan fixes

- **New** — Track your body weight and see your trend over time.
- **New** — Pick a second goal and your plan blends both.
- **New** — Exercise pages now show the muscles you are working on a body map, zoomed to the target area.
- **Improved** — A rebuilt Exercises tab: browse the full catalog right away, with simpler filters, quick access to your saved exercises, and a mini muscle map on every exercise.
- **Improved** — Cleaner exercise pages: color-coded muscle groups, and how-to steps tucked behind a tap so the demo video stays front and center.
- **Fixed** — Your plan no longer disappears when it ends. It now repeats its final week until you generate a new one.
- **Fixed** — Generated plans you have not saved yet are kept, so you can pick up where you left off.
- **Fixed** — Days only show as complete after you finish the workout.

## 2026-06-23 · Version 1.0.0 — Improvements and fixes
