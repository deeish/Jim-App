How it works

  1. Answer six questions: goal, experience, days, session length, equipment, anything to work around.
  2. Jim builds your plan. Swap any exercise you do not like for one that trains the same thing.
  3. Log sets as you go. Your history, personal bests and weekly streak build up on their own.
  4. Invite friends to a crew. Everyone's week sits in one list, no rankings, no one called out.
