Privacy Policy

Last updated 13 September 2026

Jim: Workout Planner ("Jim", "we") is an iPhone app that builds training plans and keeps your workout log. This policy says exactly what data Jim handles, why, which services process it, and how you can take it with you or delete it. It is written to match what the app actually does today. If the app changes, this page changes with it.

The short version: Jim stores the data you put into it so it can sync across sign-ins. We do not sell it, we do not run advertising or analytics trackers, and you can export or delete everything from inside the app at any time.

What Jim collects

Your account. When you sign in with email, we hold your email address. When you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive the identifier and email those services give us. With Apple you can hide your email, in which case we only ever see the relay address Apple provides. If you share your name, Jim keeps it to greet you and to show your crew. You also pick an avatar from a set of illustrations. Jim never stores a password: sign-in is by a one-time emailed code, or by Apple or Google.

Your training. Everything you enter to get and follow a plan: your goal, experience level, how many days you train, how long a session should be, the equipment you have, and any body areas you ask Jim to work around. Then the plans themselves, the workouts in them, the sets, reps and weights you log, rest days you mark, workouts and exercises you save, and body weight entries you choose to record.

Your crew. If you create or join a crew, Jim stores the crew's name and who is in it, the kudos you send, and shows the other members your name, your avatar, the days you trained this week and your session count. That is the point of a crew, and it is visible only to members.

Shares. When you share a workout, Jim creates a code. Anyone with that code can view that one workout and add it to their own account.

Diagnostics. If the app crashes or hits an unexpected error, a report is sent automatically. It contains the error, your device model, iOS version, app version and your account identifier so we can group reports. It never contains your email or your training data.

Server logs. Like any web service, our server records the IP address and timing of requests for a short period, to keep the service running and to protect it from abuse.

Jim does not read or write Apple Health, does not use your location, and does not access your contacts, photos or camera.

How Jim uses it

  • To build your plan, keep your log and sync it when you sign in on another phone.
  • To run crews and shares, which only work by showing your training to the people you chose.
  • To sign you in and email you a code when you ask for one.
  • To find and fix crashes.
  • To answer you when you write to support.

That is the whole list. Jim has no advertising, no analytics, and does not use your data to train any model.

Plans are generated with AI

When Jim builds or rebuilds a plan, it sends your training preferences (goal, experience, days, session length, equipment, areas to work around) together with Jim's own exercise catalogue to an AI provider, which returns the plan. Your name, email and logged history are not part of that request. If the provider is unavailable, Jim builds the plan from its own rules instead.

How long it is kept

Your data is kept for as long as you have an account. When you delete your account, everything above is deleted from our database immediately and your sign-in identity is removed. Provider backups roll off within 30 days and crash reports expire within 90 days. Emails you send to support stay in the support inbox.

Your controls

  • Export. Profile → Export my data gives you everything as a single JSON file.
  • Delete. Profile → Delete account removes your account and all of its data. This works for every sign-in method, including Apple with a hidden email.
  • Edit. Your name, avatar, goal, equipment and other preferences can be changed in Profile at any time.
  • Leave a crew at any time from the crew screen; your training stops being visible to its members.
  • Ask us. For anything else, including a copy of your data if you cannot open the app, email support@jimplanner.app.

Children

Jim is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly hold data about them. If you believe a child has created an account, email us and we will delete it.

Security

All traffic between the app and our services is encrypted. Sign-in tokens are stored in the iPhone's secure storage. Jim holds no passwords. No system is perfectly secure, so if we ever learn of a breach affecting your data we will tell you.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, the app will tell you the next time you open it, and the date at the top of this page will change. Smaller edits, such as adding a service that does the same job as one listed here, update the page without notice.
