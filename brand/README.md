# Jim — logo assets and spec

The mark is a letter J whose stem and hook are cut into five equal segments, so
the same shape doubles as a progress track. Same geometry everywhere; only the
colors and the dash pattern change.

Every file here is generated by `tools/generate.js` from the constants below;
edit the constants, not the files. The in-app copy of the numbers lives in
`frontend/src/lib/jimMark.ts` and is checked by `jimMark.test.ts`.

## Files

| File | Use |
|---|---|
| `jim-icon-light-1024.svg` / `.png` | Primary app icon. White ground, all five segments lit. Also `frontend/assets/icon.png`. |
| `jim-icon-dark-1024.svg` / `.png` | iOS dark icon variant. Also `frontend/assets/icon-dark.png`. |
| `jim-icon-tinted-1024.svg` / `.png` | iOS tinted icon source: white mark, transparent ground. Also `frontend/assets/icon-tinted.png`. |
| `jim-icon-mono-dark-1024.svg` / `.png` | Monochrome, for press, merch, colored grounds. |
| `jim-android-adaptive-1024.svg` / `.png` | Android adaptive foreground, mark scaled into the 66 dp safe zone. Also `frontend/assets/adaptive-icon.png`. |
| `jim-mark.svg` | Bare mark, transparent, `currentColor`, all five segments. Nav bars, lockups, favicon. |
| `jim-progress-3of5.svg` | Reference for the in-app progress element (grey track). |
| `jim-tonal-3of5.svg` | The pale-blue tonal mark. Marketing and the website hero only. |

App icons are full-bleed squares with no alpha and no baked corner radius. iOS
applies its own squircle mask, so do not round the corners yourself.

## Geometry

All coordinates are in a 100 x 100 design space. The two nested transforms center
the mark in the tile and scale it to fill about 63% of the width.

```
path      M66 20 L66 52 A22 22 0 1 1 31.38 33.98
          (stem 32 tall, then a 235° clockwise arc of radius 22 about (44,52))
stroke-width   14
stroke-linecap butt        (flat radial cuts — do not change to round)
transform      translate(50,50) scale(1.08) translate(-44,-50.5)
```

Derived constants:

```
path length     122.23
segments        5
segment length  19.25
gap             6.50
pitch           25.75
```

The hook stops at 235°, not 270°. At 270° the terminal points straight up and
the eye closes the circle (Gestalt closure), which is why the earlier draft
tested as a "C" and a "d". At 235° the terminal rises above horizontal, the
circle no longer closes, and the segments stay rectangular (19.25 x 14).

If you ever change the stroke width, keep the gap at roughly 45% of it. That
ratio is what stops the gaps closing up optically at small sizes. Verified
legible at 29 px (iOS Settings) in light, dark and tinted modes.

General form, if you need a different segment count `n`:

```
segmentLength = (122.23 - (n - 1) * gap) / n
```

## Colors

```
LIGHT (icon, in-app mark)
  background   #FFFFFF
  lit          #2563EB      theme token `colors.brand`
  tonal        #C5D8FB      marketing only

DARK (icon, in-app mark)
  background   #0B0B0B
  lit          #4D9BFF      theme token `colors.brand`
  track        #1E3663      theme token `colors.brandTrack`

MONO DARK
  background   #0B0B0B
  lit          #FFFFFF
  track        #3A3A3C

IN-APP PROGRESS, LIGHT (on white or #F2F2F7)
  lit          #2563EB      `colors.brand`
  track        #D9DDE5      `colors.brandTrack`
```

The dark variant is not a recolor of the light one. On white, pale blue recedes
and reads as "not yet." On black it becomes the brightest element and the
hierarchy inverts, so dark needs a brighter lit tone and a darker track.

`colors.brand` is deliberately not `colors.primary` (#0061C2). Primary is tuned
for 4.5:1 text contrast; the mark is tuned for presence and must match the
shipped icon exactly.

## Dash patterns by fill state

Draw two copies of the same path. The track copy underneath always uses
`19.25 6.5`. The lit copy on top uses:

```
0 of 5   (omit the lit path entirely)
1 of 5   19.25 400
2 of 5   19.25 6.5 19.25 400
3 of 5   19.25 6.5 19.25 6.5 19.25 400
4 of 5   19.25 6.5 19.25 6.5 19.25 6.5 19.25 400
5 of 5   19.25 6.5            (and omit the track copy)
```

The trailing `400` is just a gap longer than the path, so the pattern stops.

## Why the segments never measure anything

Five segments is a property of the geometry, not a count of anyone's sessions.
Plans run two to six days a week, so any mapping onto five lies next to the real
count: 1 of 2 and 3 of 6 both land on three lit segments, and 2 of 3 jumps from
three to five. A proportional mapping was built and then dropped (13 Sept 2026)
for exactly that reason. Partial fills are frames of motion only: the tap-to-rep
on Welcome and the launch animation, both of which end on 5 of 5.

## Usage rules

- The identity is the full mark: all five segments, one color. The app icon,
  the favicon, the splash, nav bars and lockups always show 5 of 5.
- The icon never shows progress state. Two tones of one blue with gaps is the
  universal grammar for "loading", and in iOS tinted mode the two tones become
  two greys. Apple's own Fitness icon shows closed rings for the same reason.
- Partial fills exist in two places only: the tap-to-rep on Welcome and the
  launch animation. Both are motion and both land on 5 of 5. Nothing in the app
  draws the mark as a meter.
- The pale-blue tonal mark is for marketing and the website hero. It never
  shows live data and never becomes the icon.
- Do not switch the caps to round. The flat radial cuts are the whole character
  of the mark.
- Do not add a gradient.
